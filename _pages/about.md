I am a PhD researcher in Electrical Engineering at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/), advised by Prof. [Volkan Cevher](https://people.epfl.ch/volkan.cevher) in the Laboratory for Information and Inference Systems (LIONS).

I work on **optimization methods for neural network training**, combining algorithm development, theoretical analysis, and large-scale language model experiments.

Before my PhD, I completed a master's in Robotics at EPFL and a bachelor's in Electrical Engineering at the University of Sarajevo.
